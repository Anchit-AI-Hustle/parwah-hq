# What comes first on Today

Today is the screen a family opens most. What is used most, and what matters
most when it is needed, comes first. Every item is scored on two things:

- **How often** a typical Indian family reaches for it (5 = several times a
  day, 4 = daily, 3 = a few times a week, 2 = a few times a month,
  1 = rarely).
- **What is at stake** if it is missing at the moment it is needed
  (5 = health, safety or money, 4 = someone you love left unsure,
  3 = a real job slows down, 2 = a nice-to-have, 1 = leisure).

The score is how often × what is at stake. A tie goes to the higher stake.

## The widgets, in order

| # | Widget | How often | At stake | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Things to do | 5 | 5 | 25 | Medicines, bills, refills, appointments: the daily work of looking after each other, and what goes wrong when it is missed. |
| 2 | People | 4 | 4 | 16 | The daily glance: has Papa checked in, where is everyone, is anyone sharing their location. |
| 3 | Chats | 5 | 3 | 15 | Used most often of all, but WhatsApp covers some of it; the floating chat button is always there too. |
| 4 | Family memory | 3 | 4 | 12 | The doctor's number, which medicine, the address: looked up often, and urgently. |
| 5 | Family vault | 2 | 5 | 10 | Aadhaar, PAN, passport, reports: rarely opened, but at a hospital or a bank it is everything. |

## The screen, top to bottom

1. **Alerts and offline notices**, when they apply: without alerts, an SOS
   does not reach this phone.
2. **The family card**: who you are with, today's status, Invite and Family
   call.
3. **For a parent in senior mode: All is well, Pills taken, SOS.** These are
   the most used and the most important buttons on a parent's phone.
4. **How are my people today?**: the daily check on each other.
5. **The widgets**, ranked above.
6. **The emergency card**: rarely opened, so it is not at the top. It is
   still one tap away and works offline.
7. **Your family**, then the set-up cards (how ready the family is, first
   steps, other families, coins).

The order is fixed rather than reshuffled by the app. People find things by
where they are, and an older hand relies on that most. What needs you right
now is already shown on the widget itself ("1 needs attention").

The gate (`npm test`, "today:") fails if this order changes without this
page changing with it.
