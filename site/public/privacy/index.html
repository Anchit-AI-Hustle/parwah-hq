Privacy Policy

Reassurance, never surveillance.

Your family's health notes, identity papers, daily tasks and locations belong to your family alone. We do not sell data, we do not show ads, and we collect only what is needed to serve your circle.

Last updated: October 2026 · Built to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, India).

Summary in plain words

1. Your family unit is private. Only members you explicitly invite into your circle can view shared notes, reminders, or papers.

2. No ad tracking in the app. The Parwah application has zero advertising trackers and zero third-party behavioural profiling.

3. Location on demand only. Location is accessed only when you choose to share it (for example during an active emergency SOS or check-in). We do not run background location tracking.

4. You can export or delete your circle anytime. When you delete a record or leave a circle, it is removed from active databases.

5. The launch list is used for the launch, and nothing else. What we keep when you join it, and how to have it deleted.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly to provide the Parwah family-care service:

  • Account information: Your mobile phone number, name, and a 4-number PIN used to sign into your account securely.
  • Family circle information: Circle names, family member names, stated relationships (such as son, daughter, mother, father), and profile notes.
  • Health facts and daily tasks: Voluntary notes you record for your family, such as blood groups, allergies, doctor contact details, insurance numbers, and daily pill or task reminders.
  • Vault records: Reference labels and numbers for family papers (such as passport, Aadhaar, or insurance policy expiry dates) entered into your vault. Access to each paper is controlled by its owner.
  • Emergency SOS and Location: If an emergency SOS is triggered, temporary coordinates and optional short audio notes are transmitted to your circle members and emergency contacts to coordinate help.

2. How We Use Information

We process your data only for genuine service purposes:

  • To keep your family circle synchronized across members' devices.
  • To deliver notifications, reminders, and alerts when daily tasks need attention.
  • To notify your designated contacts immediately if an emergency SOS is raised.
  • To verify your sign-in with your PIN and safeguard against unauthorized access.

3. No Advertising, No Data Sales

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or family data to third-party data brokers, marketing agencies, or advertisers. Parwah plans to earn through subscriptions and, once the range opens, daily-living products, never by monetizing your family records.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your records are stored securely in database servers protected by industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Authentication tokens are hashed using cryptographic functions, so plain session keys are never stored on disk.

5. Your Rights Under Indian Law (DPDP Act)

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India, you hold complete control over your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You can view all information saved in your circle directly inside the app.
  • Right to Correction: You can update, edit, or correct any record at any time.
  • Right to Erasure: You can delete individual records, remove members, or delete your entire family circle.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You may write to our designated Grievance Officer with any questions or concerns.

6. The Launch List

This part is about the form on our launch page, where you can ask to hear when the care range or the family app opens for you. It is separate from your Parwah account.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your phone number and city, only if you choose to give them.
  • What you are interested in: the care range, the family app, or both.
  • Your yes to being contacted, and the date and time you gave it.
  • Which of our pages the form was on, and when you joined or last changed your details.
  • Your internet address, for up to one day, in a counter that stops one device from sending the form again and again. It is then deleted.

The form cannot be sent until you tick the box to say we may contact you. The box is never ticked for you.

Why we collect it

To send you a message when what you asked about opens, by email or by WhatsApp if you gave a number. And to count how many people want the care range and how many want the app, so we know which to build first. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or add you to any other list.

Where it is kept

In Parwah's own database, a PostgreSQL database run for us by Neon, the same service that holds the app's data. It travels there over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Only the Parwah team can read it, and the companies that run our servers for us (Neon for the database, Vercel for the website) can reach it only to keep them running.

How long we keep it

For at most 24 months from when you last joined or changed your details. Then it is deleted automatically. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we do.

How to have it deleted

Email anchit.tandon@gmail.com from the address you joined with, with the subject "Please delete me from the launch list". We delete your details and write back to confirm within 15 working days. You can also ask us what we hold about you, or to correct it, the same way.

7. Visit Counting on Our Website

Our information pages (not the app) may ask whether we can count visits with Google Analytics. Nothing is loaded from Google until you press Allow. If you press No thanks, or your browser asks sites not to track you, nothing is loaded at all. We turn off advertising features, and Google Analytics does not store your internet address. Your answer is kept only in your own browser, and clearing your browser data asks again. The Parwah app itself never loads analytics.
