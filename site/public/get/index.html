परवाह Parwah Open the app

Put Parwah on your phone.

Open it in your phone's browser, then tap Add to Home Screen. Under a minute, its own icon, full screen, on Android and iPhone. No store needed, and it keeps itself up to date.

The easy way

Android phone

Open Parwah in Chrome, then add it to your Home Screen. It gets its own icon and opens full screen.

Open Parwah in Chrome
  1. Open parwah-hq.anchit-tandon.com in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Install
  4. Parwah is on your Home Screen. Tap it and sign in
The easy way

iPhone and iPad

Open Parwah in Safari, then add it to your Home Screen. It gets its own icon and opens full screen.

Open Parwah in Safari
  1. Open parwah-hq.anchit-tandon.com in Safari
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow pointing up)
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add
  4. Parwah is on your Home Screen. Tap it and sign in

This page is open inside another app. Tap ••• and choose Open in Safari first: only Safari can add Parwah to your iPhone.

Laptop or computer

Just open parwah-hq.anchit-tandon.com. You get the full app with a sidebar. In Chrome or Edge you can also tap the install icon in the address bar.

Why the browser? Parwah is the same app on every phone, always the newest version, with nothing to download. It is not in the Play Store or App Store yet.

Advanced options

Most families never need these. Use them only if the easy way above does not work for you.

iPhone: install with a settings profile

This adds the same Parwah icon through a profile. iPhone will say the profile is not signed. That is expected, because Parwah is not in the App Store yet. You can remove it any time in Settings, General, VPN and Device Management.

Download the iPhone profile
  1. Open this page in Safari, tap Download the iPhone profile, then Allow
  2. Open Settings and tap Profile Downloaded near the top
  3. Tap Install, enter your passcode, and tap Install again

Android: the test build

This is an early test build, not from the Play Store. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser. The browser way above is the same app and always up to date.
