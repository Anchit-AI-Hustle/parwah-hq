Put Parwah on your phone.

Installs in under a minute — its own icon, full screen, works on Android and iPhone today. Store versions are on the way; the web app is the same product, always current.

🤖 Android

Installs straight from the browser — no store needed:

  1. Open the Parwah app in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right)
  3. Tap “Install app” (or “Add to Home screen”)
Open the app to install ⬇ Direct APK download

Needs Chrome on Android 8 or newer. Google Play versionComing soon

🍎 iPhone & iPad

Installs from Safari in three taps:

  1. Open the Parwah app in Safari (not Chrome)
  2. Tap the Share button (square with ↑)
  3. Tap “Add to Home Screen”
Open the app to install

Needs iOS 16.4 or newer for the best install experience. App Store versionComing soon

If something gets stuck

I don't see “Install app”

On Android, use Chrome (not in-app browsers like WhatsApp or Instagram). On iPhone, use Safari. Visit the app once, wait a few seconds, then check the browser menu again.

It installed but looks like a website

Open the Parwah icon from your home screen — that is the installed app. Inside the browser tab it will still look like a site; that is normal.

I want to send this to Mummy-Papa

Copy the install link above, or open WhatsApp and paste https://parwah-hq.anchit-tandon.com/get/ with a short note. Their phone does the install — you do not need to be nearby.
