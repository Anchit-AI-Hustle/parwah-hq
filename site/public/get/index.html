Put Parwah on your phone.

Download the app for Android or iPhone and install it in under a minute. Its own icon, full screen, and it keeps itself up to date. No store needed.

The easy way

Android phone

Download the Parwah app and install it. It gets its own icon and opens full screen. Works on Android 7 and newer.

⬇ Download the Android app
  1. Tap Download the Android app, then open parwah.apk when it finishes
  2. If Android asks, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source, then go back
  3. Tap Install, then Open
  4. Sign in with your mobile number and PIN

Android asks once because Parwah is not in the Play Store yet. The app opens the same Parwah as this website, and updates itself with it.

Or add it from Chrome: open Parwah in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.

The easy way

iPhone and iPad

Install Parwah straight from this page. It gets its own icon on your Home Screen and opens full screen. No App Store needed.

⬇ Install the iPhone app
  1. Open this page in Safari, tap Install the iPhone app, then Allow
  2. Open Settings and tap Profile Downloaded near the top
  3. Tap Install, enter your passcode, and tap Install again
  4. Parwah is on your Home Screen. Tap it and sign in

iPhone will say the profile is not signed. That is expected, because Parwah is not in the App Store yet. You can remove it any time in Settings, General, VPN and Device Management.

Or add it yourself: open Parwah in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

This page is open inside another app. Tap ••• and choose Open in Safari first: only Safari can install Parwah on your iPhone.

Laptop or computer

Just open parwah-hq.anchit-tandon.com. You get the full app with a sidebar. In Chrome or Edge you can also tap the install icon in the address bar.

Parwah is not in the Play Store or App Store yet, so it installs straight from this page. Both apps open the same Parwah as this website, always the newest version.
