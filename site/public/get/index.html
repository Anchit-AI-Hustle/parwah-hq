Put Parwah on your phone.

Installs in under a minute from the browser: its own icon, full screen, on Android and iPhone. No store needed, and it keeps itself up to date.

🤖 Android

Download the Parwah app for Android 7 and later:

⬇ Download the Android app (7 MB) ⬇ Direct APK download

Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.

Or install it from the browser, no download:

  1. Open the Parwah app in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right)
  3. Tap “Install app” (or “Add to Home screen”)

🍎 iPhone & iPad

Install Parwah on your iPhone straight from this page: its own icon, full screen, and always up to date. Works on every iPhone and iPad, no App Store needed.

⬇ Install the iPhone app
  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Install the iPhone app, then tap Allow
  2. Open Settings and tap Profile Downloaded near the top (or General, VPN & Device Management)
  3. Tap Install, enter your passcode, and tap Install again
  4. Parwah is on your Home Screen. Tap it and sign in

Or add it yourself: open Parwah in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

This page is open inside another app. Tap ••• and choose Open in Safari first: only Safari can install Parwah on your iPhone.

For developers: the iPhone app file (IPA) installs only through Xcode on a Mac, with the install script. All app builds
