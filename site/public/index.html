Built for families who live apart

Your circle. Their choice. Care from anywhere.

Parwah is built family-first — because the person who worries and the person they worry about are usually in different cities, sometimes different countries.

Create your circle

Invite Mummy-Papa, your siblings, a trusted helper. Everyone gets the view that fits their role — and parents get an app simpler than a feature phone.

They choose what to share

Location, spends, documents — every single signal is opt-in, visible to them, and paused in one tap. Parents are the users here, never the subject.

You care from anywhere

Gurgaon, Bengaluru or Toronto — check in, book, pay, gift and talk from one place. The distance stays. The helplessness doesn't.
