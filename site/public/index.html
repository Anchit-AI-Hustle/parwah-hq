Questions families ask

Straight answers, no fine print.

Is Parwah free?

The app is free to try during beta. When Parwah Care opens, founding families pay ₹2,499/year per family (~$29 at ₹85/$) — one price for the whole circle, not per person. Nothing is charged until you choose a plan inside the app.

How is this different from WhatsApp or Life360?

WhatsApp is where families talk; Parwah is where the family knows — doctors, meds, documents, readiness. Unlike Life360, nothing is tracked by default. Parents choose every share, see who can see them, and pause everything in one tap.

Do my parents need a smartphone?

Yes, for the full app — but senior mode is built for them: big buttons, voice-first, three actions per screen. If they prefer WhatsApp, the family can still reach them there while you manage the circle.

Is location sharing mandatory?

No. Location moments are off until a parent turns them on. Parwah shows reassurance ("reached the temple"), not surveillance. Every signal is consent-first and revocable.
