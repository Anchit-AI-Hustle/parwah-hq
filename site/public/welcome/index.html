5 things, not 50

The five things a family looks for when it matters.

Each one starts from a moment you have had: the hospital counter asking for a policy number, the night nobody knew which tablets Papa takes, the bill everybody thought someone else had paid.

1

The vault

Aadhaar, PAN, policies, property papers and prescriptions, kept safely with a permission on each one. Ask someone for a paper in one tap, and only the last four digits of any number are ever stored.

2

Health and contacts

Medicines, blood group, allergies, doctors and the people to call, with the date each was last checked, so nothing out of date is trusted by mistake. Parwah reads back what is saved; it never gives medical advice.

3

To do

Book the eye check-up, renew the insurance, collect the report. Everyone sees who has what and when it is due, and when something is running late the family is told once.

4

Family

Who is who, who checked in, and where people are if they share it. Joint or nuclear, up to four generations. Say "Rohan is Papa's son" and Parwah works out Chacha, Bua, daughter-in-law, grandson. Relatives who are not on Parwah can be added by name.

5

The emergency card

Blood group, allergies, medicines and who to call, on one screen a doctor can read. It works with no internet, because the moment it matters is the moment the signal is worst.

And, when you want them

The rest sits under More, out of the way.

Ekta, the family helper

Ask what is saved for anyone, prepare a doctor visit, or check whether a message is a scam. If someone is unwell right now, she tells you to call 112 and press SOS, at once.

Plans, calls and games

Plan a trip or a puja, start a family video call, and play Ludo, Get Away or Snakes & Ladders together across cities.

The day and location

Muhurat and the day's timings for your city, and location sharing that each person turns on for themselves, for as long as they choose.
