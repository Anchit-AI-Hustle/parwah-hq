About Parwah

Parwah (परवाह, "care") is a free family-care app made in India. It gives a family one shared place for the everyday work of looking after parents: a daily "I'm okay" check-in, shared to-dos, the family's doctors, medicines and emergency card, important papers, an SOS button that alerts and calls the family, video calls and games. Parents use a calm, large-text mode and stay in charge: they switch on the safety features that concern them, and nobody joins a family without being let in. It is built for the many Indian families who live in different cities or countries from their parents.

Founder: Anchit Tandon, founder.

Key facts

  • Free for families. There is no charge today. A paid Family Plan is coming later.
  • Made in India, in English with Hinglish, for families in India and Indians abroad.
  • Works in the browser on Android, iPhone and computers. Open it, then Add to Home Screen. It is not in the app stores yet.
  • Family circle with approval. People join with a code, and someone already in the family has to say yes.
  • Consent first. A parent turns on location sharing themselves, and stopping deletes the position. Parwah is the family together, with the parent's yes.
  • Daily check-in. One tap says "All is well" to the whole family.
  • Family book and vault. Doctors, medicines, allergies, insurance and an emergency card that works without internet. ID papers keep only the last 4 digits of a number.
  • SOS alerts the family and offers one tap to 112. It is not an emergency service.
  • Ekta, the family helper, answers questions from the family's own records and checks a suspicious message for scams, free. She gives no medical advice.
  • Together: family video calls, Ludo, Vyapar and Antakshari, and to-dos you can say in Hinglish.
