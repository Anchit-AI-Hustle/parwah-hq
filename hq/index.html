The thesis

Buy the catalog. Build the brand. Own the child-buyer.

White-label the entire senior daily-living catalog — already manufactured at scale by Tynor/Vissco-class OEMs, nutra CMs and ODMs — wrap it in a dignity-first brand sold to the 35–50 metro/NRI adult child, and take the empty "care for the people who raised you" slot before AGEasy or a funded entrant does. Zero R&D. Trust is the product.

Why now

  • Demand curve: India 60+ ≈ 138M (2021) → 319M by 2050. Compounds for 25 years.
  • Market being educated for free: services layer funded — Age Care Labs ₹85 Cr Series B1 (Rainmatter), Primus, Samarth, Portea.
  • Product layer unbranded: one corporate entrant (AGEasy diaper pants, Mar '26); Seniority (~$1M raised) absorbed by HCAH 2022. No digital-first indie brand.
  • Supply is ready: the full catalog exists as OEM/ODM white-label — this is a curation + trust + distribution play.

Operating posture

  • Hard gates, pre-decided — G1–G3 and an M6 kill rule (see Gate Rail). No funding losses past the gate.
  • One deliverable closed at a time; test everything before handover; dual currency ₹ + $ @ ₹85.
  • Never fight on diapers — enter incontinence phase 2 via hygiene CM once trust exists.
  • Next action: P0 sourcing shortlist — named OEM contacts, MOQs, target landed costs (master prompt first action).

04 · Product spec

PRD — the buyer ≠ user commerce system

The digital product that sells the physical catalog: storefront, gift-to-parent flow, no-login subscriptions, WhatsApp concierge, analytics. v1 targets: CAC ≤ ₹700, gift-flow ≥ 25% of orders, 60-day repeat ≥ 4%, senior served with zero app dependency.

Personas & jobs

  • P1 Buyer-child (primary): 35–50, metro/NRI, time-poor, guilt-motivated. "Help me tangibly care for Mum/Dad from far away, without a research project."
  • P2 Senior end-user: 60+, WhatsApp-capable, app-averse, dignity-sensitive, COD-leaning. "Let me stay independent without feeling like a patient."
  • P3 Caregiver: manages daily use, low English. "Show me exactly how to use this, in Hindi, in 60 seconds."

Non-goals v1

Native app · services marketplace · international checkout · incontinence SKUs · Class C/D devices · AI medical-advice chat (hard exclusion).

Must-have stories (v1)

  • Ship-to-different-address with NRI prepaid smooth (US1)
  • Free printed gift card with personal message (US2)
  • Problem-first bundles — "Bathroom safety kit", "Knee relief kit", "Diwali care box" (US3)
  • QR → 60-sec Hindi/English how-to video on every product + delivery (US4)
  • Opt-in WhatsApp thread: setup help, reorder, Hindi callback (US5)
  • Subscribe at −10% via magic link, no login (US6)
  • COD for India + 10-day no-questions returns, WA pickup (US7)

Should (v1.1): caregiver accounts (2 parent profiles), festival/birthday auto-reminders, expandable "why we chose this" physio notes. Could (v1.2): q-commerce sync, referral credits, Hindi storefront.

Functional blocks F1–F6

  • F1 Storefront (Shopify): problem-first nav (Mobility / Bathroom Safety / Pain & Comfort / Daily Aids / Nutrition / Gifting); PDP = ≤30s hero video, in-use senior imagery, load-rating spec, doctor badge, "completes the kit"; Razorpay + PayPal (NRI).
  • F2 Gift flow: gift toggle → 240-char printed message → delivery-day "your child sent you this" WA/SMS → buyer gets delivery photo.
  • F3 Subscriptions: Appstle-class app, magic-link manage, dunning 2 retries + WA nudge, cancel reasons captured.
  • F4 WhatsApp concierge (WATI-class BSP): order status · how-to D+1 · satisfaction D+7 · replenish D+30/45 · human handoff ≤ 2 hrs (10:00–19:00 IST) · Hindi + English.
  • F5 Reviews & trust: D+10 ask, ₹100 photo-review credit, hosted NABL/load-test certs per SKU.
  • F6 Tracking: GA4 + Meta CAPI + server-side; canonical events incl. add_gift_message, wa_optin, wa_reorder, subscribe_start; UTM discipline; weekly KPI board.

Non-functional

WCAG 2.1 AA (≥16px base, 4.5:1 contrast, keyboard nav) · LCP ≤ 2.5s on 4G mid-range Android · zero therapeutic claims with ASCI gate · Legal Metrology fields on PDP · DPDP consent for WA · 99.5% uptime · order→WA latency ≤ 5 min.

Success metrics (90 days, descending)

CVR ≥ 1.6% blended (≥ 2.4% gifting LPs) → CAC ≤ ₹700 / MER ≥ 2.2 → gift-flow ≥ 25% → WA opt-in ≥ 55%, video open ≥ 60% → 60-day repeat ≥ 4% → returns ≤ 6%, RTO ≤ 12% of COD.

Releases & open questions

v0.1 (W3–4) internal → v1.0 (W5–6) public with gift flow + concierge + tracking → v1.1 (M3) subscriptions + caregiver profiles → v1.2 (M6–7) Hindi + referrals + q-commerce sync. Open by W2: trademark clearance + fallback name; OEM license coverage letter; PayPal vs Razorpay-intl test; ₹50L liability cover bound before first safety-SKU sale.

How the confusion gets resolved — evidence, not opinion

Signals to collect M1–M6

  • Repeat direction: do grab-bar buyers return for supplements (row-wise) — or ask "do you have this for my kid/dog" (column-wise)?
  • Language: share of reviews/support tickets saying "for my mother/father" vs product-generic ("good knee support").
  • WA concierge inbound: % of tickets requesting other-user products.
  • CAC by territory: does the child-buyer emotional angle keep beating problem-generic creative?

Pre-decided rules
