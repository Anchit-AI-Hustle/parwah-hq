# RWA and senior-club session: "Phone smart, family close"

A 45-minute, hands-on session for residents' welfare associations, senior citizens' clubs, laughter clubs and temple or gurdwara committees. Parents come with their phones; adult children are welcome. The session is useful on its own even for people who never use Parwah.

**Who runs it:** Anchit or a trained volunteer, plus one helper for every 8 to 10 people.
**Bring:** a phone stand or projector, printed one-page "how to" cards (large text), a spare charger, sign-in sheet with a consent box for follow-up.
**Language:** Hindi and English, mixed as the room prefers.
**Code for any link shared:** `?ref=RW1&utm_source=rwa&utm_medium=session&utm_campaign=<society-name>`

## Outline

| Time | Part | What happens |
|------|------|--------------|
| 0 to 5 min | Welcome | Chai, names, "Who here has a son or daughter in another city or country?" Hands go up. |
| 5 to 15 min | Spot the scam | Read out five real-style messages: the electricity cut, the parcel fee, the KYC link, the "I am your grandson, send money" call, the refund OTP. The room votes: real or fake? Explain the three signs: hurry, money, a link or OTP. Share **1930**, the national cyber crime helpline, and cybercrime.gov.in. |
| 15 to 25 min | Your phone, your way | Bigger text on the phone, how to save an emergency contact, how to share a photo of a paper on WhatsApp. |
| 25 to 40 min | Parwah, hands-on (optional) | For those who want it: open Parwah in the browser, Add to Home Screen, the big buttons (SOS, All is well, Medicines taken). Each person decides if they want to join their family; helpers help them invite their children. Show Ekta's free scam check with one of the messages from earlier. |
| 40 to 45 min | Questions and close | Leave the large-text card. Nobody's phone is set up without them saying yes, and nobody is asked for any money. |

**Rules for the room:** nobody touches a phone without the owner's yes. We never ask for an OTP, a PIN or a bank detail, and we say so out loud, because that is the lesson.
