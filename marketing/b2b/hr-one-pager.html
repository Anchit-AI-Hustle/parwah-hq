For HR, People and Benefits teams

A caregiving benefit for the quiet second job.

Many of your people look after a parent while they work: from the next city, from another state, or from another country. They book check-ups between meetings, chase insurance papers at lunch, and take the 11pm call. It rarely shows up in a leave request, but it shows up in focus and in late-night stress.

What Parwah gives a family

A free family-care app, made in India. One shared place for the whole family:

  • A daily check-in. The parent taps "All is well", and everyone sees it.
  • Shared to-dos that show who has each one, with reminders and repeating tasks.
  • The family book: doctors, medicines, allergies, insurance and an emergency card that works offline.
  • A document vault that keeps only the last 4 digits of an ID number.
  • SOS that alerts the family and gives one tap to 112. It is not an emergency service.
  • Video calls and games, and Ekta, a helper that answers from the family's own records and checks scam messages for free.

Parents use a calm, large-text mode and stay in charge: they choose what to share, and nobody joins a family without a yes.

Why it fits a benefits programme

  • Free to the employee today. No procurement needed to start a pilot.
  • Private by design. The employer never sees anything inside a family.
  • Works on any phone, in the browser, with Add to Home Screen.
  • Made for Indian families, in English and Hinglish.

A simple pilot

  1. A 30-minute session for employees: "Looking after parents from a distance". Practical, no sales pitch.
  2. A one-week setup drive: we help employees set up their families, including a parent's phone over a video call.
  3. A short survey at 4 weeks that you own, so you decide on your own data.
