## 2. Tue 10 Nov, Bhai Dooj: the sibling who remembers everything

Happy Bhai Dooj.

In most families I know, one sibling quietly carries the care. They know the doctor's name, the date of the next check-up, where the insurance papers are, and which pharmacy delivers. The others help when asked.

That sibling is tired. Not because they mind, but because it all lives in their head.

Parwah makes it a shared list. Invite your brother or sister, and the to-dos, doctors, medicines and bills are in one place for both of you. Each task shows who has it, so nobody has to ask "did you do it?" on the family group.

Today's gift idea: invite your sibling, and take one thing off their list.

## 3. Thu 12 Nov: why we built Parwah consent-first

When you hear "family safety app", the first thought is often: "So I can see where my parents are?"

Only if they choose to share it.

We made some decisions early, and we will keep them:

- A parent switches on location sharing themselves. Stopping deletes the position. It is not just hidden.
- Nobody joins a family without someone already in it saying yes.
- ID papers keep only the last 4 digits of a number.
- The app itself carries no advertising trackers.

Our parents raised us, ran homes and careers, and made every big decision in our lives for decades. A family app should not quietly take that away from them. Parwah is the family together, with the parent's yes.

Reassurance, never surveillance.

Where do you draw the line between caring and watching?

## 4. Tue 17 Nov: flying home in December?

If you live abroad, you probably already know the dates. Mid December to mid January, the flight home, the weddings, the long lunches.

And the last evening, when you sit with Mummy-Papa and try to sort out everything you will not be there for.

This year, use that evening for one thing: set up Parwah together.

- Add your parents to the family and give them the big-button screen.
- Put the family doctor, the medicines and the emergency card in one place.
- Snap the insurance papers into the vault.
- Make the first family video call from their phone, so they know how.

Then fly back knowing the whole family can see the same list, from Dubai, Toronto or Delhi.

It takes one evening, and it is free. What is on your list for the trip home?
