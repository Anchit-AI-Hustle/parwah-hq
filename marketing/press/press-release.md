## Parwah launches a free, consent-first family app for Indians caring for parents from a distance

*Families share check-ins, to-dos, doctors and papers in one place, while parents stay in charge of what they share*

**[CITY], 3 November 2026.** Parwah (परवाह, "care"), a family-care app made in India, opened to the public today, free for families. Parwah gives the adult children, parents and siblings of a family one shared place for the everyday work of looking after parents, whether the family lives in the same city, across states or in another country.

Every morning, a parent can tap one large button, "All is well", and the whole family sees it. Families keep shared to-dos that show who has each task, the family's doctors, medicines, allergies and insurance details, an emergency card that works without internet, and important papers in a vault that stores only the last four digits of an ID number. An SOS button alerts the whole family at once and offers one tap to 112. Ekta, a helper inside the app, answers questions from the family's own records and checks a suspicious message for scams, free of charge. Families can also make video calls and play Ludo, Vyapar and Antakshari together.

Parwah is built consent-first. Parents use a calm, large-text mode and switch on the features that concern them, such as location sharing, themselves; stopping deletes the shared position. Nobody joins a family without someone already in it saying yes.

"Every one of us knows the 11pm phone call that ends with 'sab theek hai, beta'," said Anchit Tandon, founder of Parwah. "You hear the words, but not the week: the check-up that moved, the bill, the strange call asking for an OTP. Parwah gives the whole family one place for that, and our parents stay in charge of it. It is not about keeping watch. It is about being there together."

Parwah works in any phone's browser on Android and iPhone, and on computers. Families open parwah-hq.anchit-tandon.com and tap Add to Home Screen to keep it like an app. It is free for families; a paid Family Plan with extra features is planned for later.

Features coming this month include a gentle alert to the family when a day's check-in is missed, an "ask family first" button for calls about money or OTPs with one tap to 1930, the national cyber crime helpline, and reminders for annual pension life certificates.

Parwah does not give medical advice and is not an emergency service.

### About Parwah

Parwah (परवाह) is a free family-care app made in India, for families who look after their parents together, from near or far. It brings check-ins, shared to-dos, the family's health contacts and papers, SOS alerts, video calls and games into one place, built consent-first so parents stay in charge.
