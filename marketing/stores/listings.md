## Google Play

**App name** (30 max): `Parwah: Family Care Together` [28]

**Short description** (80 max): `Daily check-ins, shared to-dos and family papers. Parents stay in charge. Free.` [79]

**Full description** (4000 max):

```
Parwah (परवाह) is a free family-care app, made in India. It gives your whole family one shared place to look after Mummy-Papa together, from the next street or from another country. Parents stay in charge.

ONE TAP: ALL IS WELL
Your parent taps one big button, and the whole family sees it. No call needed to ask, no call needed to answer.

A CALM SCREEN FOR PARENTS
Big, clear buttons: SOS, All is well, and Medicines taken. Large text. Nothing else to learn.

SHARED TO-DOS
Check-ups, bills, gas refills, renewals. Give a task to someone and everyone sees who has it. Repeating tasks and reminders. Say a to-do out loud in Hinglish and Parwah writes it down.

THE FAMILY BOOK
Doctors, medicines, allergies, blood groups and insurance details in one place the whole family can read. An emergency card with the details a doctor will ask for, which works without internet.

A SAFE PLACE FOR PAPERS
Aadhaar, PAN, licence and policy papers in a family vault. Only the last 4 digits of an ID number are kept, and each person chooses who can open their papers.

SOS FOR THE FAMILY
One tap alerts everyone in the family at once and offers one tap to 112. Parwah is not an emergency service.

EKTA, YOUR FAMILY HELPER
Ask about anything your family has saved. Paste a suspicious message and Ekta checks it for scams, free. Ekta gives no medical advice.

TOGETHER, FROM ANYWHERE
Family video calls, plans and a shared calendar. Play Ludo, Vyapar and Antakshari with the family.

CONSENT FIRST
Nobody joins your family without someone in it saying yes. Location sharing is off until a person turns it on themselves, and stopping deletes it. Parwah is the family together, with the parent's yes.

FREE FOR FAMILIES
Parwah is free for families today. A Family Plan with extras is coming later.

Made with parwah, in India.
```
