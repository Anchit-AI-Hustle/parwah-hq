# Creator brief: "I set this up for my Papa"

## Who we want

- Creators aged roughly 28 to 45 whose audience is adult children: working professionals in Indian metros, and Indians abroad (UAE, US, UK, Canada).
- Real family content already on their page: parents, siblings, festivals at home, the trip home. Their parents are happy to appear (their own yes, on camera, before we book).
- Formats: Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn video. English, Hinglish or Hindi.
- Size: nano and micro creators first (about 5,000 to 100,000 followers). Trust matters more than reach here.
- Not a fit: pages built on health advice, fear, pranks on parents, or "how I keep tabs on my family" content.

## The story

**"I set this up for my Papa."** One honest moment from the creator's life:

1. **The hook (0 to 3s):** the 11pm call, or packing to fly back. "Every call ends with 'sab theek hai'..."
2. **The real problem (3 to 10s):** the bit nobody says: the doctor's number in one phone, papers in a drawer, the sibling who remembers everything.
3. **Setting it up together (10 to 25s):** sitting with their parent, opening Parwah in the browser, adding it to the Home Screen, the parent pressing the big "All is well" button themselves. The parent is shown as capable and in charge, doing it with the creator, not having it done to them.
4. **The feeling (25 to 35s):** what changed. "Now my sister and I see the same list." "Papa sends me his All is well before his walk."
5. **The ask:** "It's free for families. Link in bio / in the comments."

Only say what really happened in your family. If your parent did not use a feature, do not show it.

## Must say

- Parwah is a **free** family app, made in India.
- **Parents stay in charge:** they choose what to share; nobody joins the family without a yes.
- It works in the phone's browser; tap **Add to Home Screen**. It is not in the app stores yet.
- At least one real thing it does, from this list (all live today): the daily "All is well" check-in; shared to-dos that show who has each one; the family doctors, medicines, allergies and insurance in one place; the emergency card that works offline; SOS that alerts the family and gives one tap to 112; family video calls; Ludo, Vyapar and Antakshari; Ekta's free scam check; to-dos by voice in Hinglish.
- The disclosure (see below).

## Never say

<!-- never-say -->
- Any health claim. Never: cure, treat, heal, therapy, remedy, medicinal, prevents, reverses, clinically proven, doctor recommended, pain relief, guaranteed results. Never name a condition (for example arthritis or diabetes).
- Never "elderly", "burden", "old people" or anything that pities parents.
- Never "monitor", "track", "keep tabs on", "spy on" or "watch over" your parents. Parwah is the family together, with the parent's yes.
- Never say SOS calls an ambulance or replaces 112. It alerts the family.
- Never say a medicine tick is medical advice or proof a dose was taken. It is a record the family keeps.
- Never compare Parwah with, or name, any other app or company.
- Never mention features as live if they are "coming this month" (quiet-day alert, ask family first, dose nudges, hospital mode, life-certificate reminder, call rota, home staff register, shared expenses). You may say "coming soon" only if we confirm it in writing.
- Never mention a price other than "free for families"; a paid plan is "coming later", no date, no amount.
- No hospital scenes, no frail or sad imagery, no scary "what if something happens" framing.

## Disclosure (ASCI, required on every paid post)

The disclosure goes **in the caption itself, in the first two lines**, so it shows before "more". A hashtag at the end is not enough. Use the platform's paid-partnership label too, but the words in the caption are required either way.

- Instagram / YouTube Shorts caption line 1: `Paid partnership with Parwah` (or `#Ad` at the very start).
- On the video itself: the word **Ad** or **Paid partnership** on screen for the first 3 seconds, readable on a phone.
- LinkedIn: start the post with `Paid partnership with Parwah.`
- If you got the product free and were not paid, say `Gifted by Parwah` the same way. Parwah is free for everyone, so this applies only to extras like travel.

Example caption:

> Paid partnership with Parwah. Every call with Papa ended with "sab theek hai". So this Diwali we set up Parwah together 🪔 Now my sister and I see the same to-do list, and Papa taps "All is well" every morning. It's free for families, link in bio. #Parwah #Ad

## Approval

1. **Brief call** (15 minutes): the story, the parent's yes to appear, which features are real for this family.
2. **Script or outline** sent to anchit.tandon@gmail.com at least 3 days before filming. We check it against the lists above and reply within 1 working day.
3. **Draft video and caption** before posting. We check claims, the disclosure, and that the parent is shown with dignity. Up to 2 rounds of changes, only for accuracy and compliance, never to make it less like you.
4. **Go live** on the agreed date. Send the post link the same day.
5. **Day 7:** share the reach and saves screenshot; we share how many families your link brought.

We can ask for an edit or removal if a post breaks the never-say list. Your parent can ask for removal at any time, for any reason, and we will support it.
